# Q3 2026 notes for the board

Hollin Valley Cargo Bikes (fictional company, Desk Test data). Notes from the CEO, Priya Hale, for the Q3 board meeting on 14 October 2026. Monthly figures are in metrics.csv.

## How the quarter went

- Best quarter so far. Revenue grew every month and September was our first month above $1.7 million.
- We launched the Hollin Duo longtail in August. It made up 21% of September units.
- The dealer partnership with Riverside Cycle Collective went live in July. Their 14 stores sold 88 bikes in the quarter.
- Gross margin kept improving as the new frame supplier's pricing kicked in.
- Customer acquisition cost fell each month while we spent more on marketing, because referrals picked up.

## What went wrong

- A hub-motor supplier delay in August left about 60 orders on backorder. All but 9 shipped by the end of September.
- Warranty claims rose to 3.1% of bikes sold, up from 2.4% in Q2. Most claims are the rear rack mount, which we redesigned in September.
- We missed our hiring plan by two people in service and repair.

## Risks

- Cash: we are still burning cash while we build inventory for spring. Burn has been steady, so runway is comfortable, but a bigger spring build would shorten it.
- One supplier makes all our hub motors. A second source is being qualified and should be approved in Q1 2027.
- Warranty costs if the rack redesign does not fix the claims.

## What we need from the board

1. Approve a $1.2 million inventory line of credit for the spring build.
2. Approve four new hires: two assembly technicians, one service lead and one marketing manager.
3. Agree the Q4 target of $5.6 million revenue.

## Q4 outlook

- Q4 is usually our quietest quarter for consumer sales, but dealer orders for spring arrive in November and December.
- Target: $5.6 million revenue, gross margin at or above 40%.
