This archive preserves eleven public pages about the Marrowby town council plan to replace its diesel bus fleet with battery-electric buses. Each entry lists the page title, its canonical source address, and the date it was saved. The pages were captured on the same day from their original public addresses and are reproduced here unchanged so that the facts they contain can be checked against one another. The collection spans official council material (the cabinet report, committee minutes, a budget breakdown and a planning notice), local press coverage (a news report and an opinion column), a vendor specification sheet, an independent research summary, a national grant announcement, a residents' forum discussion and a rider FAQ. Where a figure appears on more than one page, the canonical council and grant sources should be treated as authoritative over press, forum or vendor restatements of the same number.
