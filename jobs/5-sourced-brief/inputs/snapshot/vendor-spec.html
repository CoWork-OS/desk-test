Voltline V12 — battery-electric city bus

The Voltline V12 is a single-deck, low-floor battery-electric bus designed for urban and suburban town routes. This sheet summarises the headline specification for fleet buyers.

Length12.0 m single deck

ChargingOvernight depot charging, 150 kW; full charge in approx. 4 hours
List price£465,000 per vehicle (fleet configuration)

Drivetrain and efficiency

The V12 uses a central electric motor driving the rear axle, with regenerative braking that recovers energy on the stop-start duty typical of town services. Voltline rates the vehicle at 280 km of range under a standard duty cycle measured in mild conditions; range in service varies with temperature, terrain, load and driving style. Buyers planning cold-climate operation should model range against their own winter conditions.

Maintenance

With fewer moving parts than a diesel driveline, the V12 is designed for reduced scheduled servicing. Voltline offers an eight-year battery warranty and a depot-side diagnostics package.
