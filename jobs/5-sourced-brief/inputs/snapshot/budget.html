Notes on the figures

The unit price of £465,000 is the fleet-configuration cost quoted by the preferred vehicle supplier and is held flat across the Phase 1 order of 12 buses. The diesel comparison of £240,000 is the current like-for-like replacement cost and is shown for reference only; no further diesel vehicles are included in this programme. The depot figure of £3.8 million is a single capital line covering grid reinforcement, charging units and civil works at Kestrel Lane, and is not expected to recur in later phases.

Of the £9,380,000 Phase 1 capital requirement, the national clean-transport grant of £6.2 million meets the larger share, with the £3.18 million balance drawn from the council's own capital programme. The grant is paid in tranches against delivery milestones, so the council's contribution front-loads early spending until grant tranches are released. Phases 2 and 3, which will replace the remaining vehicles toward the 2029 target, are not costed here and will be presented in future budget rounds.

Operating case

Once the full fleet is electric, the Finance service projects savings of £1.1 million per year on fuel and maintenance combined. These recurring savings underpin the programme's whole-life business case and are not included in the Phase 1 capital totals above.
