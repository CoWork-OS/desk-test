National Clean Transport Fund — 2026 round two awards

The National Clean Transport Fund supports local authorities replacing diesel public-transport fleets with zero-emission vehicles. The following awards were confirmed in round two of the 2026 programme. Each award is tied to a published decarbonisation plan with a dated target for a fully electric fleet.

Marrowby award

Marrowby is awarded £6.2 million toward its programme to replace a fleet of diesel buses with battery-electric vehicles and to upgrade its depot for overnight charging, supporting its target of a fully electric fleet by 2029. The award is conditional on the authority meeting the Fund's standard reporting requirements and delivering against the milestones in its approved plan.

Conditions

All awards are paid in tranches against delivery milestones. Recipients must report annually on vehicles in service, energy use and emissions avoided. Funds not committed within the programme period must be returned. The Fund does not cover ongoing operating costs, which remain the responsibility of the local authority.
