Planning notice: Kestrel Lane depot charging upgrade

Application ref: PL/2026/0218 · Notice posted 3 March 2026

Proposal

Notice is given of an application by the council's transport service to upgrade the bus depot at Kestrel Lane to enable overnight charging of battery-electric buses. The works are required to support the town's electric bus transition and the Phase 1 delivery of 12 electric buses in 2026.

Description of works

The application covers installation of overnight charging infrastructure within the existing depot footprint, including charging units at parking bays, associated cabling, a substation enclosure for grid reinforcement, and minor resurfacing. The upgrade is costed at £3.8 million. No extension to the depot building or increase in the number of parking bays is proposed.

Hours and impact

Charging would take place overnight while buses are stabled, typically between 23:00 and 05:00. The council states that the substation enclosure has been sited and screened to limit noise to neighbouring properties, and that daytime depot operations and local traffic movements are not expected to change materially.

Timing

If consented, the works are programmed to complete before the first Phase 1 vehicles are delivered, so that charging capacity is available from the start of service. The council notes that the depot was selected as the hub for the first electric routes precisely because the affected services return to Kestrel Lane overnight.

How to comment

Representations on this application may be made in writing to the planning service within 21 days of the date of this notice, quoting the application reference above. All comments received within the consultation period will be reported to the determining committee.
