Transport committee — minutes

Meeting held 11 February 2026, Council Chamber. Present: nine members. In the chair: Councillor R. Halloway.

Item 4 — Electric bus transition programme

The committee considered the cabinet report (ref TRA-2026-07) recommending replacement of the fleet of 38 diesel buses with battery-electric vehicles, with a target of a fully electric fleet by 2029.

The Director of Environment and Transport introduced the item. Members noted that Phase 1 would see 12 electric buses ordered for delivery in 2026, and that the Kestrel Lane depot would require a charging upgrade costed at £3.8 million. A member asked whether the depot works could begin before vehicle delivery; officers confirmed the works were scheduled to complete ahead of the first buses arriving.

Discussion

Councillor Pell raised concern about winter range and asked that performance be monitored once the first vehicles entered service. Councillor Adeyemi welcomed the programme but asked officers to report annually on actual against projected savings. The chair agreed both points would be added to the monitoring schedule.

A member queried the phasing of later orders. Officers confirmed that only Phase 1 was being approved for funding at this meeting, with Phases 2 and 3 to return in future budget rounds as the route-by-route rollout continued toward the 2029 target.

Decision

The committee resolved to recommend the programme to Cabinet, including the Phase 1 order of 12 electric buses and the Kestrel Lane depot upgrade.

Vote: 7 in favour, 1 against, 1 abstention. Carried.

The meeting closed at 20:05. The next ordinary meeting of the committee is scheduled for 15 April 2026.
