# Interview 10: Senior nurse and staff rep, three-vet clinic

Fictional interview for the Desk Test. Date: 2026-09-23. Interviewer: R. Okafor.

**Interviewer:** You're coming at this from the staff side rather than management. Can you explain your role?

**Participant:** Yeah, I'm a senior nurse at a three-vet clinic in Glennister, and I'm also the staff rep, so when people have a moan about the rota, it lands on me. There are fifteen of us. We got Tidemark about eight months ago, and I wasn't part of choosing it.

**Interviewer:** What's the most common moan?

**Participant:** That shifts change and nobody knows who did it. Like, I'll open the app on a Sunday and my Wednesday late has become a Wednesday early. Is that our manager? One of the vets? Did I agree to it and forget? The app just shows the new shift as if it had always been that way.

**Interviewer:** How does that make people feel?

**Participant:** Kind of powerless, honestly. When a schedule rewrites itself with no fingerprints on it, people stop trusting any of it. I've had colleagues screenshot their rota every Monday just so they can prove what it said before.

**Interviewer:** They screenshot it as evidence?

**Participant:** As evidence, yeah. One of the auxiliaries had her hours cut by a shift and she was convinced it was personal. It turned out the manager had adjusted it for a staff training day and forgot to say. A single line saying "moved for CPD day, by Sarah" would have saved a week of bad atmosphere.

**Interviewer:** How do you hear about changes, generally?

**Participant:** We're supposed to get notifications. The problem is the app sends so many that they've become wallpaper. It tells you when someone else's break moves, when a comment's added, when the month is "published," which happens about four times because people keep republishing. I'd guess I was getting thirty a day at one point.

**Interviewer:** What did you do about it?

**Participant:** Turned them off, like most of us did. And then I missed that my Saturday had been cancelled and drove in for nothing, forty minutes each way. So now I'm in this silly situation where I check the app manually three times a day because I can't trust the alerts and I can't live with them either.

**Interviewer:** If you could set the rules for notifications, what would they be?

**Participant:** Only tell me about my own shifts. Tell me once, clearly, with who changed it and why. Everything else can wait in the app until I go looking.

**Interviewer:** Do you feel management takes these concerns seriously?

**Participant:** Our manager does, she's actually been pushing Tidemark about it. But she can only see what the app shows her too. The staff aren't asking for control over the rota; we're asking to be able to read it like adults.

**Interviewer:** Anything else?

**Participant:** Just that the people who use it most, nurses and receptionists, weren't the people it was designed for. You can feel that every time you open it.
