# Interview 02: Owner-vet, single-vet practice

Fictional interview for the Desk Test. Date: 2026-09-03. Interviewer: R. Okafor.

**Interviewer:** Thanks for talking with me. Could you describe your practice?

**Participant:** It's me, essentially. One vet, two nurses, a receptionist and a half, a kennel assistant, and then two relief vets I bring in when I need a day off or I'm doing a farm visit. Little place on Main Street in Marrowford. We've had Tidemark for eleven months now.

**Interviewer:** What made you try it?

**Participant:** My wife, frankly. She used to do the rota on Sunday nights and she said she'd divorce me before she did another one. That's a joke. Mostly.

**Interviewer:** What's been the most useful part?

**Participant:** The rota templates, without question. I built a "normal week," a "school holidays week" and a "me on leave week," and now I just stamp one onto the calendar and tweak two or three shifts. The templates are the reason I renewed. Before, the rota ate about four hours of our Sunday; now it's twenty minutes, tops.

**Interviewer:** Four hours down to twenty minutes, every week?

**Participant:** Near enough. Some weeks there's a public holiday and I fiddle more. But I'd say I've got back the better part of a working day each month, and at my age I count those. Honestly I'd pay for the templates on their own and ignore the rest of the product.

**Interviewer:** Is there anything you'd pay less for, then?

**Participant:** The seats. Ha. Look, it's $9 per person per month, which isn't ruinous. But my two relief vets might do three days between them in a quiet month. Why should Hamid, who comes in twice a quarter, cost me the same as Lorna, who's here five days a week?

**Interviewer:** Have you raised that with Tidemark?

**Participant:** I did, in an email. I got back a very polite paragraph about how every seat gets "full platform value." Well, Hamid doesn't get full platform value. He gets a text telling him which Thursday he's working.

**Interviewer:** Did you consider removing the relief vets from the system?

**Participant:** I tried it for a month, actually. Took them out, kept them on a sticky note on my monitor. And of course the one day I forgot to check the sticky note, I double-booked myself for a cat spay and a lameness workup at the same time. So they went back in, and I went back to paying.

**Interviewer:** If you could change one thing about the pricing, what would it be?

**Participant:** Charge me per shift for relief people. Or give me three "occasional" seats free with the plan. A per-seat model makes sense for a corporate group with sixty full-timers; for a one-vet shop with locums it's a tax on being small.

**Interviewer:** Last question. Would you recommend it?

**Participant:** I already have, to two other owners at the regional meeting. I told them: buy it for the templates, grit your teeth at the invoice.
