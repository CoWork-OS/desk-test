# Interview 01: Practice manager, three-vet clinic

Fictional interview for the Desk Test. Date: 2026-09-02. Interviewer: R. Okafor.

**Interviewer:** To start, can you tell me a bit about the clinic and how long you've been on Tidemark?

**Participant:** Sure. We're a three-vet small-animal practice in Quillbrook, seventeen people on the books if you count everyone, and we went live on Tidemark about three months ago. Well, "went live" is generous. We signed up three months ago.

**Interviewer:** What do you mean by that?

**Participant:** The connection to Vetrolume, which is our practice-management system, took almost five weeks to work properly. The sales call made it sound like you click a button and the appointment book flows across. It did not flow. It dripped, and then it stopped.

**Interviewer:** What went wrong, as far as you could tell?

**Participant:** Honestly, I'm still not totally sure. The sync kept dropping our consult room codes, so every vet showed up as "unassigned" on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Their support person was kind, but every fix came back as "try reconnecting," and I must have reconnected it a dozen times. I'm a practice manager, not an integrations engineer.

**Interviewer:** And bringing your staff in, how did that go?

**Participant:** Oh, the staff import. So you upload a spreadsheet, right? Except their spreadsheet wanted contracted hours in one column and roles in another, and ours had them mashed together because that's how our old paper rota worked. I spent two evenings at my kitchen table retyping seventeen people. If I'd known, I'd have budgeted a full week for setup instead of the afternoon they promised.

**Interviewer:** Now that it's running, is it worth it?

**Participant:** Mostly, yes. Once it was in, building the month takes me maybe ninety minutes instead of half a Friday. I won't pretend it isn't better than the whiteboard.

**Interviewer:** Let's talk about cost. How is that structured for you?

**Participant:** It's $11 per seat per month, and that's where I get grumpy. Four of my seventeen are part-timers, and one of them, Dot, works Saturday mornings only. She's in the building maybe sixteen hours a month and she costs the same to schedule as a full-time vet.

**Interviewer:** How does that land with the owners?

**Participant:** The owners look at the invoice and ask why we're paying full price for someone who's barely there. And I don't have a good answer. Paying a full seat for a Saturday-only receptionist feels like being charged for an empty chair.

**Interviewer:** What would feel fair to you?

**Participant:** Something tied to hours, or a cheaper tier for anyone under, say, twenty hours a week. I'd even accept a flat clinic price. What I don't want is to start leaving people out of the app just to save eleven bucks, because then the schedule is wrong, and a wrong schedule is worse than no schedule.

**Interviewer:** Anything you'd tell another manager who's about to start?

**Participant:** Clean your staff list before you touch the import, and don't believe anyone who says the Vetrolume link takes an afternoon. Ask them for a named person who will stay with you until the first full month runs.
